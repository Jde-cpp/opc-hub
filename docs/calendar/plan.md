# Calendar implementation plan - 2026-10-19

A plan for [`spec.md`](spec.md) (2026-10-19), whose open questions were ruled and whose review ([`calendar-spec-review.md`](../../../reviews/calendar-spec-review.md)) was folded the same day; this plan's own review ([`calendar-plan-review.md`](../../../reviews/calendar-plan-review.md), 12 findings) was folded 2026-10-20. It has the shape of the servers plan ([`../servers/plan.md`](../servers/plan.md)) and the historian's ([`libs/historian/docs/plan.md`](../../libs/historian/docs/plan.md)): the spec's pieces in dependency order, each phase a PR or two with its own tests, and three milestones that merge end to end. Status is tracked on GitHub: [#391] is the parent, each phase below names its issue, whose body is the plan's text at filing and is refreshed from here after a fold, as the servers plan's are, and the milestones are [I](https://github.com/Jde-cpp/opc-hub/milestone/8), [II](https://github.com/Jde-cpp/opc-hub/milestone/9) and [III](https://github.com/Jde-cpp/opc-hub/milestone/10).

Line numbers cite opc-hub2 at `4bf17829`, main with the gateway refactor merged, as the spec does.

Three things the spec's piece table does not say:

- **The shared ladder is merged.** The spec's review (#7) asked for the AppServer's mutation steps, which `HubQL` re-spelled, to move into an `App::Server::CustomMutation` both QLs call. The gateway refactor ([`gateway-refactor.md`](../../../reviews/gateway-refactor.md)), merged 2026-10-19 as PR #390, has done it: [`AppServerQL.h:13`](../../apps/AppServer/src/ql/AppServerQL.h#L13) declares `App::Server::CustomQuery` and `CustomMutation`, `GatewayQL.h` the gateway's pair, and `HubQL` calls them in place of the arms it re-spelled ([`HubQL.cpp:13`](../../apps/OpcHub/src/ql/HubQL.cpp#L13), [`:20`](../../apps/OpcHub/src/ql/HubQL.cpp#L20)). No phase builds it: `CalendarMAwait` and `calendarOccurrences` join those arms. The merge also dropped `AppQLAwait.h` and `.cpp`, whose query ladder is now `Server::CustomQuery` in `AppServerQL.cpp`, so the spec's *Validation* and *Reads*, which cited both, were re-cited against it when this plan's review was folded, and Phases 2 and 3 branch from main at any time after Phase 1. The refactor also gives `IAppClient` `Enroll` and `GrantRights` and no reconnect seam, so `OnReconnected()` stays Phase 3's.
- **No Phase 0.** The one prerequisite the review turned up is merged, and the two small changes outside the library, the reconnect seam and `timeZone` on `status`, mean nothing without their consumers and ride with Phases 3 and 2.
- **Piece 4 is two phases**, the pages and the grid. The tab, the detail page's Properties, Pattern list and Exceptions and the help topic are one PR of the historian admin's shape (#337); the grid, *Change from…* and the Preview are a component of its own with its own tests. The first is useful before the second: a site's zone, day start, shifts and holidays can all be entered from the pages, and the grid draws what they entered.

## Risks

- **Cites against a moving main.** Both documents cite `4bf17829`, main after the refactor merged; the lines Phases 2 and 3 lean on, the ladder's arms and `Gateway::Connect`, moved once already and are the likeliest to move again before they are built, so each phase's PR re-reads them.
- **The reconnect path is untested above `libs/app`.** `reloadAccess` runs on a replay and nothing drops a socket in a test; Phase 3's reconnect case is the first and may find the access reload's own faults. Fix them in that PR or file them.
- **Fan-out to the hub's in-process client.** The client's subscriptions in the hub sit where the AppServer's own listener's do and the stock mutation publishes to them; Phase 3's hub test is the proof, and if the in-process path drops them the fallback is the await calling the cache directly, which only the hub can do.
- **Two reloads at once.** Events inside the debounce restart one timer; a reload still running when the timer fires again is followed by one more, not two, and the map swaps once under its lock.
- **The time zone database.** libc++ reads `/usr/share/zoneinfo`, and the package depends on `tzdata` for it ([`build-deb.sh:231`](../../apps/OpcHub/setup/linux/build-deb.sh#L231)); MSVC's chrono reads ICU's, and the historian's suite resolves `America/New_York` on both in CI already. A name one side has and the other lacks: the form offers the browser's list, and the await's refusal is the final word.
- **The grid's size.** Piece 4 is L and 4b is the open-ended half. It draws what the spec draws; crews, recurring exceptions and iCal are the spec's *Later*.
- **Spec drift.** A PR that changes behaviour changes [`spec.md`](spec.md) with it.
