# Demo site implementation plan - 2026-10-21

A plan for [`spec.md`](spec.md) (2026-10-21), whose open questions #1 to #11 were ruled as recommended the same day, #12, the name and the host, ruled by the owner 2026-11-15 (0.3), and whose review's thirteen findings ([`demo-spec-review.md`](../../../reviews/demo-spec-review.md)) were applied and folded the same day. It has the shape of the Open Protocol plan ([`../open-protocol/plan.md`](../open-protocol/plan.md)) and the OEE plan ([`../oee/plan.md`](../oee/plan.md)): prerequisites that are each useful alone, then the spec's pieces in dependency order, each phase a PR with its own tests, and three milestones that merge end to end. Its review's nineteen findings ([`demo-plan-review.md`](../../../reviews/demo-plan-review.md)) were ruled fix as recommended and folded 2026-10-21, into the spec too where they touch it, and into the servers plan's Phase 6 for #2 and #18. Its issues were filed 2026-10-21: the parent [#476], label `demo`, in milestones Demo I, II and III, each phase below naming its own, whose body is the plan's text at filing and is refreshed from here after a fold, as the sibling plans' are; 0.4 is a private draft item on the project, as security work is.

Line numbers cite opc-hub2 at `af6ea641`, main with the web and OpcServer refactors merged, as the spec does. Refs re-anchored on 2026-10-23, the UA server library's move among them, cite branch `servers-1a-ua-server-library`.

Seven things the spec's piece table does not say:

- **The hub goes public before the plant exists.** Pieces 5, 6 and the deployment's box need nothing of the servers, OEE or calendar plans, so milestone I is the hub at its public name behind nginx with a guest on its pages and the admin signed in through the tunnel, with no server connection yet. That starts the listener's soak months before a PLC exists, behind 0.4's release and review, and the smoke's HTTP half proves the posture on day one.
- **Piece 1 is two phases.** The production mode's random, timed and slow stops are emulator-only and test in its suite with no server, after the OEE plan's Phase 7 ([#407]); the nodeset and the five overlays are a PLC-side change that runs only on `-transport=server`, after the servers plan's Phase 6 ([#387]). The first is Phase 1, the second Phase 2.
- **Piece 7 is three PRs.** The box, the tarball with its units, the hub overlay, the nginx site and the seed's access half, lands in milestone I; the plant's seed, which needs Phases 2 and 3 and the OEE and calendar rows, in milestone II; the operations and the opening last.
- **The seed's identity is its own.** The spec had the seed log in "as the emulator's `-grant` does" and the admin sign in "as the owner the seed made"; those are two users, and the spec now says so. The seed makes a certificate of its own, `demo-seed`, which the demo overlay trusts, and grants that user `owner` first; the admin's Google user does not exist until the first sign-in through the tunnel, after which `seed.sh grant <email>` grants it `owner` from the runbook (Phase 8).
- **Piece 6 is M.** The fold gave it the port rule for every in-process instance, the trusted proxies, the sign-in scripts loaded on demand and the policy, which is three codebases; the spec now sizes it so.
- **The line-status fan-out was not in the OEE plan.** The spec asks the OEE plan's Phase 8 ([#408]) to compute a line's status once per change and send it to every subscription of that line; that plan's Phase 8 read per push. It was folded there and into [#408]'s body at filing (0.2); Phase 4 here carries it only if #408 lands without it.
- **Two small gaps ride along.** The shipped `viewer` lacks the historian's four tables and `http.threads` is read by nothing, which the spec names and files nowhere; the first goes with Phase 6, which edits the roles file, and the second with Phase 7, which edits the settings.

Each device holds a random engine of its own, a `std::mt19937_64` seeded from a new device key, `seed`, else from the start time, drawn through `absl::Exponential` and `absl::Uniform`: the shared `Math::BitGen()` is seeded from the OS per thread ([`mathUtils.h:21`](../../include/jde/fwk/utils/mathUtils.h#L21)), and a seeded `absl::BitGen` is salted per process, so neither repeats a sequence across runs. `speed` is a tag of the `follow` mode ([`Signals.cpp:73`](../../apps/OpcServer/emulator/Signals.cpp#L73)) whose target is the production mode's current rate rather than `ratedRpm`, the cycle under the `slow` factor in force, or 0 in a stop, lagging by its `tau`. The README's config table ([`README.md:89`](../../apps/OpcServer/emulator/README.md#L89)) gains the keys and [`Opc.PlcEmulator.jsonnet:89`](../../apps/OpcServer/emulator/config/Opc.PlcEmulator.jsonnet#L89)'s pumps keep #407's `{every, for, code}`.

Tests, in the emulator's suite ([`DevicesTests.cpp`](../../apps/OpcServer/emulator/tests/DevicesTests.cpp), [`SignalsTests.cpp`](../../apps/OpcServer/emulator/tests/SignalsTests.cpp)), which links the emulator's library whole ([`emulator/tests/CMakeLists.txt:22`](../../apps/OpcServer/emulator/tests/CMakeLists.txt#L22)), on the device clock the suite advances by hand ([`Emulator.cpp:32`](../../apps/OpcServer/emulator/Emulator.cpp#L32)), never the wall clock: over a fixed `seed`, a thousand random stops' gaps average near `mean` and every length is inside `[min, max]`; two devices with different seeds differ and the same seed repeats, as it does in another run; an `at` stop starts at its time in its zone on a day with a clock change; a `slow` period stretches the cycle by its factor and `speed` lags toward the slowed rate, then back; a coded stop followed by an uncoded one leaves `reasonCode` 0 for the second; a `zone` that does not resolve and a `max` below `min` are refused at parse. Built 2026-11-15, where it differs from the text above:

- **`speed` names its count.** A `follow` tag takes a new key, `follows`, the device's `production` tag whose pace it lags towards, in units a minute, where `ratedRpm` would be (ruled 2026-11-15). A name that is no `production` tag of the device is refused, and so is the key on another mode.
- **`code` may be left out** on all three shapes, 0, a stop with no reason (ruled 2026-11-15). #407's refusal of a `code` of 0 is gone.
- **`zone` is UTC where left out**, shown hidden in the stock config (ruled 2026-11-15).
- **A rate, not a switch.** A generator is sampled with the share of its pace the device ran at ([`Signals.h`](../../apps/OpcServer/emulator/Signals.h)): 0 off, 1 on, 1/`factor` in a slow period. `production` works that share of the time and `follow` lags towards that share of its target, so the pumps' `motorRpm` would slow too, had they a `slow` list.
- **One window per entry.** An entry of `stops` or `slow` holds the window it is in or the next ([`Devices.h`](../../apps/OpcServer/emulator/Devices.h)), and moves on when the device clock reaches its end, in the lists' order, so a seed draws the same windows whatever the period.
- **A random gap runs from the last one's end**, the first from the start. The length is drawn on the clock's own ticks, so `min` and `max` are both reached.
- **`at`:** `06:00` or `06:00:00`. An entry under way at the start is in force from it. A time the clocks skip is the instant they jump to and one they repeat its first, as `Chrono::ToTimePoint` reads a local time. `for` is under 23 hours, the shortest day a clock change makes: a longer one would run into the next day's window on the day the clocks go forward.
- **No `seed`:** the start time plus the device's place in the list, so two devices of one process differ. The seed is logged at startup.
- **Refused besides:** an entry with no shape or two, a key of another shape's (a `factor` on a stop, a `code` on a slow period), a `mean` or `min` that is not positive, a `factor` that is not over 1, a `slow` list on a device with no `production` tag, and a `ratedRpm` beside `follows`.
- **The wall clock is read each period.** Each cycle anchors the device's start to it, and the `at` windows move with it, so a pause the steady clock misses, a suspend or a paused VM, doesn't shift them. The other shapes keep the device clock, which is the suite's.
- **Overlapping stops:** any holds `status` off, and the first coded one gives `reasonCode`, so an uncoded stop never hides a coded one's reason.
- **`zone` is looked up only when set or for an `at` entry**, so a host without tzdata runs the rest. A name that does not resolve is refused by `Chrono::ToTimeZone` ([`chrono.h`](../../include/jde/fwk/chrono.h)), the historian's lookup too, and tested in `ChronoTests`.
- **A `counter` and a `randomWalk` step once a period**, though the stops' and slow periods' edges cut it into pieces.
- **The seed's case pins two stops' times**, to the millisecond, which is what another run means in one process. An abseil that draws a distribution another way moves them.
- By hand, 100 s under `-transport=server` against a hub on a scratch sqlite database: an `at` stop at its New York second with its code, a coded random stop, `motorRpm` with `follows` at 60 a minute, and at 30 with the count at half its pace through a `factor` of 2.

## Risks

- **Three other plans' pace.** Milestone II waits on the servers plan's milestone II, the OEE plan's milestone III and the calendar's host; milestone I waits on none of them, which is why it goes first, and Phases 1, 3, 4 and 5 can each start on their prerequisite's branch if it has to.
- **The first public listener.** Nothing of the hub has faced the internet; 0.4's release and pre-auth review, the firewall held to the admin until it closes, the limits, the headers and the soak are the defence, and the opening is a checklist, not a date.
- **The guest's reach.** Every read a visitor may make is a role grant the seed writes; a resource added by a later plan ships unenforced, and the seed's last step enforces what exists at the time. The smoke's refusals are the regression test, and the runbook's upgrade step re-runs the seed's `access` half, which grants the visitor's reads that now exist and enforces anything new. The PLCs' users need Read on `access.users` once it is enforced, which `demo-plc` gives.
- **The port behind the front.** The page dials the port it came from only through `config.json`; a stale copy in a browser's cache, or the page answered for a missing file, sends `/opc` to 1967. `no-cache` and the JSON-only fallback are the guard, and Phase 7's spec covers both.
- **nginx and the sockets.** A proxy timeout below the sockets' 300 s drops every visitor's feed at once; the file sets both timeouts above it, and the smoke's wait on the Filler would see a drop.
- **Timing in the suites.** Random gaps of 40 minutes and stops at a shift's start are hours on a line and must be the device clock's in the suite, so Phase 1 takes `zone` and `seed` and the suite moves the clock; nothing waits on the wall clock, and the emulator's suite stays inside the 300 s ctest timeout ([`functions.cmake:180`](../../build/functions.cmake#L180)).
- **The soak's numbers.** An OEE between 60 and 85 percent is tuned, not designed; the figures live in the overlays so a restart is all a change costs, and the week is for that.
- **Certificates on the box.** Six client certificates, five channel certificates and the hub's own must each sit in a directory the right list names before the first login, or a PLC runs with no session and the collector sees nothing; `seed.sh certs` runs before the PLC units start, under their `StateDirectory`, and the runbook says so.
- **The policy.** Angular's inline styles and the build's inlined critical CSS need `'unsafe-inline'`; a release that changes how the build inlines turns a clean report-only soak into a blank site. The soak re-runs on every deploy before the policy is enforced, and `ngCspNonce` is the spec's *Later*.
- **Let's Encrypt.** A renewal that fails goes unnoticed until the certificate expires; the outside check's TLS validity is the alarm, and certbot's timer logs to the journal the runbook says to read.
- **Spec drift.** A PR that changes behaviour changes [`spec.md`](spec.md) with it; the seed's identity, the three-PR split and piece 6's size were folded into it with the plan's review, and the fan-out is the OEE plan's.
